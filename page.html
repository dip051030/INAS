About us

Dedicated to the welfare of Nepalese artists and the promotion of Nepalese culture in the UK

International Nepalese Artist Society UK CIC is a non profit organisation. By organising performing arts programmes that express the rich cultural heritage of Nepal, we create opportunities for Nepalese artists to showcase their talents and share their stories.

01

Supporting Artists

Through our initiatives, Nepalese artists gain valuable exposure, allowing them to build their careers while preserving and promoting the unique cultural values of Nepal.

02

Building Community

We foster a sense of community by bringing people together, strengthening cultural ties, and providing a supportive network for Nepalese creatives living in the UK.

03

Celebrating Heritage

We create spaces where cultural identity is celebrated and the richness of Nepalese traditions is shared with future generations, enhancing cultural understanding and diversity in the UK.
