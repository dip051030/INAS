The 12th INAS Europe Regional Programme 2027

Two days of Nepali song and dance in south-east London: the INAS Europe regional meeting, and the 12th European Solo Nepali Dance Competition with the 9th European Solo Singing Competition — 26 competitors from 12 countries, plus about 30 INAS representatives from Europe and the Global committee.

28–29 March 2027 Woolwich & Plumstead, London SE18

Everything happening around the event

INAS Europe Regional Meeting

Progress reports, presentations, guest speeches and future plans.

For INAS national committees, artists, community leaders and guests

European competitions

Solo dance and solo singing with 26 competitors from 12 countries and an expert judging panel.

For performers from across Europe and the public audience

Language & heritage workshops

Workshops in Nepali folk song and dance, taught in Nepali and English by experienced artists.

For children, young people, families and anyone interested

Talent development

UK heats and mentoring for emerging UK-based singers and dancers, leading to places in the European competition.

For emerging artists in England, Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland

Recording & sharing

Professional filming of the competitions, published on the INAS UK YouTube channel with English captions.

For online audiences in the UK, Nepal and worldwide

The full project timeline

November 2026Application submittedGrant application sent to Arts Council England.

Beyond 2027

A free online archive — the competition recordings stay online as a free resource with English captions.
A regular youth programme — the UK heats and workshops are planned as the basis of an ongoing INAS UK youth programme.
Bigger future events — the partnerships and skills built by hosting will support future applications and public programmes.

Dates, venues and audience figures are planned and remain subject to venue capacity and event arrangements. Organised by International Nepalese Artist Society UK CIC (no. 15988250).
